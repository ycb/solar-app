II. Full Racking & Array capture plan

A) Attachments / penetrations and flashing

Inspector is looking for: proper flashing/boot, no obvious roof damage, attachment spacing/cantilever plausibility, workmanship.

Capture moment: After attachments & flashing are installed, before modules cover the view.

Capture package
	1.	Photos – “Every attachment, close + context”

	•	Type: Photos (multiple)
	•	Angle:
	•	Close-up: 12–18 inches away, perpendicular to flashing/penetration
	•	Context: step back 3–6 feet to show attachment location relative to rail line
	•	What “good” looks like (acceptance checks):
	•	Flashing fully visible, not cut off
	•	No blur; edges sharp
	•	Roof material around penetration visible (tiles/shingles)
	•	If tiles: show any tile replacement/fit around flashing

In-app guidance pattern
	•	“Take 2 photos per attachment: close-up + context.”
	•	Thumbnail grid grouped by “Attachment #” (or just count-based with “Add another”).

⸻

B) Rail alignment and module plane

Inspector is looking for: straight/level rails, consistent module plane, no obvious sag/cantilever issues, general workmanship.

Capture moment: After rails and modules are installed.

Capture package
2) Video – “Edge sweep along each array side”
	•	Type: Video (10–20s each side; 2–4 videos depending on array shape)
	•	Angle: low edge-on sweep, parallel to module plane
	•	Path: walk the long edge slowly; keep horizon steady
	•	What “good” looks like:
	•	Entire edge stays in frame
	•	Consistent gap/plane visible (no obvious warping)
	•	No rapid shaking; stable movement

Optional helper (prototype-level)
	•	A simple overlay: “Keep the array edge centered” + “Walk slowly”.

⸻

C) Fire access pathways and perimeter setbacks (visual proof)

Inspector is looking for: clear pathway zones and edges (the exact inches vary by AHJ), but practically they need a clear, contextual view that pathways exist and are not obstructed.

Capture moment: After modules installed.

Capture package
3) Video – “Top-down sweep showing array + roof edges”
	•	Type: Video (10–20s)
	•	Angle: looking down at modules + roof boundary; include ridge/eave/edges in frame
	•	Path: slow pan that includes roof edge → pathway → array
	•	What “good” looks like:
	•	Roof edges are visible, not just modules
	•	The gap/pathway is clearly seen in relation to edge/ridge
	•	Lighting is sufficient to see boundaries

If you want a fallback
	•	Add an option: “Take 2 photos: ridge line view + eave/edge view” if video quality fails.

⸻

D) Module condition and clamps/fasteners

Inspector is looking for: no cracked modules, clamps present and seated, consistent installation quality.

Capture moment: After module installation.

Capture package
4) Photos – “Representative clamp + module condition set”
	•	Type: Photos (not every clamp; representative sampling)
	•	Angle:
	•	1 photo per array corner (4) showing module edges + clamps
	•	1 close-up of mid-clamp and end-clamp seated properly
	•	What “good” looks like:
	•	Clamp hardware fully visible
	•	No glare washing out hardware
	•	Module edges intact and visible

Prototype note
	•	Present this as “Capture 6 photos” rather than “capture all clamps.”

⸻

E) Wire management on roof (MLPE, trunk cable, clips)

Inspector is looking for: cables secured, not resting on roof, connectors seated, general neatness.

Capture moment: After wiring complete, before leaving roof.

Capture package
5) Photos – “Wiring + connector evidence”
	•	Type: Photos (3–6)
	•	Angle:
	•	2–3 photos under-array/edge showing cable routing and clips
	•	1 close-up of a representative connector seated
	•	1 context shot showing no loose cable on roof surface
	•	What “good” looks like:
	•	Cable supports/clips visible
	•	Roof surface visible where you claim clearance
	•	Connectors in focus

Important constraint
	•	Under-array visibility may be limited; allow “best possible view” + prompt to capture from array edge.
